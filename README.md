# Dua Mode

A guided, step-by-step dua drawn from the Qur'an and authentic hadith. It follows the order the Prophet ﷺ taught (Tirmidhi 3477): praise Allah first, then send salawat on the Prophet ﷺ, then ask.

**Open it:** https://mohammedsohail-virufy.github.io/dua-mode/

## What's inside

1. Adab of dua
2. Exalting Allah: tasbih, the best words of the Prophets, the two duas with Allah's Greatest Name, the dua of Yunus
3. As-Salat al-Ibrahimiyyah
4. Sayyid al-Istighfar
5. Asking for all good, including the dua for everything the Prophet ﷺ asked for (Tirmidhi 3521)
6. Refuge from all bad
7. Your own dua (general prompts)
8. Closing (As-Saffat 37:180–182)

Every dua shows its source.

## Languages and modes

- **Languages:** English, Urdu (اردو), Tamil (தமிழ்), Arabic (العربية, full Arabic interface)
- **Translation:** Arabic with the full meaning below
- **Word by word:** the meaning under each Arabic phrase
- **Arabic only:** large Arabic text for reciting

Links remember the language and mode, so you can share a ready-set page:

- Tamil, word by word: `?lang=t&view=words`
- Urdu translation: `?lang=u&view=tr`
- Full Arabic: `?lang=a`
- Arabic only: `?view=arabic`

Keys: Space or → for next, ← for back, C to count. On a phone, swipe.

## Corrections

The translations and word-by-word meanings are being reviewed. If you find a mistake in the Arabic, a translation or a reference, please [open an issue](../../issues) or send a pull request. All content is in [`duas.js`](duas.js).
